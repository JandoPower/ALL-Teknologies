About

All Teknologies LLC

All Teknologies is a technology company built out of the operating side of business. We work with owner-operated companies — construction, real estate and services — that have outgrown spreadsheets and subscriptions that almost fit.

Some of what we build we take on as a client project. Some of it, like Invoicing, we run ourselves and offer as a service. Either way it gets used in a real business before we put our name on it.

Your data stays yours

Every account is isolated at the database level, with private file storage and per-company access control. Nobody sees another business's books.

Built for two countries

We work in English and Spanish, with systems set up for both United States and Honduras entities — the right tax fields, formats and language for the people using them.
